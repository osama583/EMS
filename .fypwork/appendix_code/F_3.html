45 
46 NO credential offered -> guest, and the endpoint serves its public tier. That is the whole
47 reason this exists rather than authenticate().
48

50 swallowed and treated as a guest, on the reasoning that a public endpoint should never refuse
51 anyone. That was wrong in a way that was invisible from the server: an expired access token is
52 what a signed-in user sends, so their request came back 200 with the signed-out answer, and
53 because it was never a 401, auth.interceptor.ts never ran its refresh-and-replay - the one
54 mechanism that recovers an expired session. The user stayed silently downgraded to a guest for
55 the life of that tab while every other page in the app refreshed normally and worked.
56 
57 On /ai/ask that surfaced as a wrong authorization answer, not just missing personalisation: a
58 guest holds no assignments, so ai/topic_access.py denied `clubs` (and kept `events`, its one
59 a guest-open topic, now ai/scope.py's VISITOR areas) and the assistant told a signed-in student "an administrator has not granted
60 your role" about pages their role is in fact granted - see tests/test_auth_wiring.py.
